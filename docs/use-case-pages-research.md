## Content boundaries

All interactive records are fictional examples. Shared addresses do not establish ownership; discrepancies do not prove fraud. Financial examples use transparent CAD arithmetic: 120,000 inflows less 25,000 transfers and 15,000 financing equals 80,000 operating receipts; (100,000 − 70,000) / 15,000 equals 2.00× cash-flow coverage; 6,000 − 3,500 − 1,500 equals 1,000 household residual cash.

Processing completion is distinct from institutional approval. AMLOK notifications go to registered systems, not borrowers. No servicing case creation, task assignment, payment collection, promise management, or borrower outreach capability is claimed. Verification, calculation, and investigation remain included stages of Insights or Signal.

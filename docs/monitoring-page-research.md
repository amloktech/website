Editorial synthesis: show concrete changes, distinguish use cases, and connect monitoring findings to a human review. Competitor metrics, automated actions, proprietary assets, and coverage claims were not reused as AMLOK capabilities.

## Illustration definitions

All charts and dashboard graphics are labeled illustrative. CAD 100,000 to CAD 80,000 receipts is a 20% decrease; receivable concentration from 30% to 45% is a 15-percentage-point increase. Graph traces are schematic, not empirical time series. The income example is an absent observation, not proof of unemployment. The source disruption example intentionally leaves evidence missing instead of interpolating a financial result.

No guaranteed lead time, loss reduction, live refresh SLA, universal graph access, automated servicing action, or insurance policy-administration capability is asserted. Insurance scope is financial monitoring for trade credit, surety, and premium finance. Change-driven reassessment is included in Signal; a separate requested full underwriting is distinct.

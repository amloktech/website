## Illustration boundaries

All diagrams and output previews are illustrative. The cash-flow slider uses CAD 100,000 monthly receipts, CAD 70,000 operating outflows, and CAD 15,000 scheduled debt service. Coverage is (receipts − outflows) / debt service. A 20% receipt decline produces 0.67× coverage and −CAD 5,000 remaining cash. Costs and debt service remain constant. This is a mechanical sensitivity example, not a forecast, approval threshold, or validation result.

Institutions retain decision authority. Insurance examples concern financial evidence for trade credit, surety, and premium finance, not a claim that AMLOK replaces policy administration, actuarial pricing, or claims decisions. Scope and outputs are agreed for the institution's workflow.
